Your quests

How points work

First verified new card: +10 XP / +2 coins. Condition assessment: +10 XP / +2 coins. Daily quest: +25 XP / +5 coins. Weekly challenge: +100 XP / +20 coins. Repeat scans and imported existing collections: 0 points. Card-related activity is capped at 30 XP/day; daily quests at 100 XP/day. These are proposed production rules; this demo awards only the daily and weekly quest points, not unverified scans or card claims.

Proposed year-end Top 10 prizes
